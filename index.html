Dive Bars

West Asheville, almost all of it strung along one stretch of Haywood Road. Cheap drinks, real locals, no craft-beer lecture. You can walk the whole run — and I'll pick you up when you're done.

West Asheville · 375 Haywood Rd

Double Crown

The one I send people to first. Low light, red glow, soul and country 45s on the turntable. Feels like a bar that's been there fifty years even though it hasn't.

Open in Maps →
West Asheville · 504 Haywood Rd

Desoto Lounge

Pinball, ping-pong, foosball, a good jukebox and a hidden back patio. Kitchen runs late, which matters at midnight. Easiest room in West for a group.

Open in Maps →
West Asheville · 496 Haywood Rd

Fleetwood's

Vintage shop, dive bar, and a working Vegas-style wedding chapel under one roof. Punk and rock shows most nights. People genuinely do get married here at 11pm.

Open in Maps →
West Asheville · 1045 Haywood Rd

The Odd

Formerly the Odditorium. Punk, metal, drag, burlesque, and murals on every surface. Open till 2am. Not a quiet-conversation bar — that's the point.

Open in Maps →
West Asheville · 777 Haywood Rd

Westville Pub

The true neighborhood pub of the strip — pool tables, trivia, live music, cheap pints, open 10:30am to 2am every day. Brews their own now as All Sevens.

Open in Maps →
West Asheville · 784 Haywood Rd

Universal Joint

Right across the street from Westville. Burgers, tater tots, and one of the biggest patios in West. Where the strip goes when the weather's good.

Open in Maps →
West Asheville · 507 Haywood Rd

The Whale

Half a step up from a dive — a tiny beer bar with a constantly rotating list of imports you won't see anywhere else in town. Good last stop before the ride home.
